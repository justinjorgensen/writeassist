Foundation

Core Configuration Files

The essential files that configure and control your entire writing ecosystem

🔗 How They Work Together

These three files form the foundation of WriteAssist. Author-rules defines your boundaries, story-compendium holds your content, and project-config configures AI behavior for your specific project. Together, they ensure every agent and command works in harmony with your vision.

Constraints
author-rules.md
Your creative law book
What It Does

Your living manifesto that defines what must never happen in your story. CRITICAL: NO EM DASHES EVER (zero tolerance policy). Also bans head-hopping, deus ex machina, and other violations. Defines what to avoid unless justified (like dream sequences) and what to always include (like sensory grounding). It evolves based on lessons learned, creating an ever-smarter set of guardrails that keep your writing consistent and professional.

How It Connects

Every AI agent reads this file FIRST before any task. When the rule-enforcer agent detects patterns, it suggests updates. Commands like generate-wrp and execute-wrp check rules before creating content. This ensures your creative vision is respected in every word written, making AI feel like a trained assistant who knows your preferences.

Updated By:
update-rules command
Reference
story-compendium.md
Your story's encyclopedia
What It Does

The single source of truth for everything in your story world. It tracks every character's details and arc, maintains your timeline and chronology, documents all locations and settings, maps your plot structure, and records themes and symbols. As your story grows, this document grows with it, preventing continuity errors and forgotten details.

How It Connects

Continuity-checker validates new content against it. Character-developer references it for voice consistency. World-builder adds new locations to it. Timeline-keeper updates chronology in it. Every writing command checks facts here before generating content. This is the master document that keeps your 300-page novel as consistent as a 3-page short story.

Location:
WriteAssist/story-compendium.md
Project Settings
project-config.md
Your project configuration
What It Does

Configures how AI agents behave in your specific project. It defines your genre, target audience, writing style preferences, and quality standards. It tells AI whether to write literary or commercial prose, how much description to include, what themes to emphasize, and how to handle sensitive topics. This makes AI adapt to YOUR voice rather than using generic responses.

How It Connects

Every agent and command reads this after checking author-rules.md. Style-editor uses it to match your voice. Chapter-writer references it for tone. Market-analyst considers your audience settings. This configuration ensures that whether you're writing romance or horror, literary or commercial, the AI assistance matches your intended style and market.

Configures:
All 23 AI agents
💡 Best Practice

Start by customizing author-rules.md with your specific constraints, fill out story-compendium.md with your story details, and fine-tune project-config.md for your genre, voice, and themes. These files are your control panel. The more detailed they are, the better your AI assistance becomes.
