## Scene Structure

### Scene 1: The Morning Routine (500 words)
- **Purpose**: Establish normal, show character
- **POV**: Sarah, third person limited
- **Setting**: Sarah's apartment, 7 AM
- **Conflict**: Subtle wrongness (David's absence)
- **Elements**:
  - Cold coffee she made for two
  - Empty side of bed
  - Checking phone repeatedly

### Scene 2: The Delivery (1000 words)
- **Purpose**: Inciting incident
- **POV**: Sarah, third person limited
- **Setting**: Same apartment, 7:30 AM
- **Conflict**: Divorce papers arrive
- **Elements**:
  - Courier at door
  - Legal language shock
  - David's signature already there
  - Discovery of affair evidence

### Scene 3: The Response (1200 words)
- **Purpose**: Character revelation through action
- **POV**: Sarah, third person limited
- **Setting**: Apartment to office
- **Conflict**: Internal vs. external composure
- **Elements**:
  - Call to best friend
  - Decision to go to work
  - Subway scene showing inner turmoil
  - Arrival at office

### Scene 4: The Decision (800 words)
- **Purpose**: Setup for story ahead
- **POV**: Sarah, third person limited
- **Setting**: Office bathroom to elevator
- **Conflict**: Stay or go?
- **Elements**:
  - Mirror moment
  - Text from David
  - Choice made
  - Chapter ending hook

## Success Criteria
This chapter succeeds if:
1. Reader empathizes with Sarah immediately
2. Divorce feels shocking but inevitable in hindsight
3. Sarah's complexity shows (not just victim)
4. Reader needs to know what happens next
5. Setting and tone established clearly
