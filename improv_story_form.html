Improv Story Builder

Collaborate to create a unique story prompt for WriteAssist

Pick the emotional shape of the story. This guides the beginning, middle, climax, and ending.

• Hero's Journey: growth through trials
• Rags to Riches: rise from nothing
• Tragedy: downfall from flaw or fate
• Rebirth: renewal or redemption
• Voyage and Return: leave, learn, return
• Overcoming the Monster: face a looming threat
• Comedy: chaos resolves into harmony
• Transcendence: sacrifice or enlightenment
• Fall and Rise: crash, then comeback

Pick one or more flavors. Genres set expectations for mood, pacing, and tropes.

Example mixes: Sci-Fi + Comedy, Fantasy + Mystery.

Where and when does it happen? Add one vivid detail.

Example: Derelict mining ship orbiting Jupiter with frozen windows that sing.

Each person adds one character: name, role, quirk or secret. One per line.

Example: Dr. Imani Reyes, xenobiologist who hears the rocks sing.

What kicks things off. The spark disrupts normal life and forces action.

Examples: a strange visitor arrives; someone vanishes; a disaster begins.

Optional twists, constraints, or surprises. Use for callbacks, secrets, or a wildcard event.

Example: Once per round someone may shout Wildcard to add a surprise portal or betrayal.
