## Project Context

**Project Name**: The Lighthouse Keeper
**Genre**: Literary short fiction
**Target Audience**: Adult readers of quiet, character-driven stories
**Writing Stage**: Drafting

## Writing Style Guidelines

### Voice & Tone
- **Narrative Voice**: Third person limited, past tense
- **Tone**: Restrained, melancholic, hopeful at the edges
- **Formality Level**: Literary
- **Emotional Range**: Restrained, with controlled release

### Prose Preferences
- **Sentence Variety**: Mixed lengths, short lines for impact
- **Description Style**: Balanced, sensory but spare
- **Metaphor Usage**: Occasional, drawn from sea and weather
- **Pacing**: Deliberate

### ALWAYS DO
- Maintain a single consistent point of view per scene
- Ground each scene in concrete sensory detail
- Earn emotional moments through action, not statement
- Keep the sea present as mood, not decoration

### NEVER DO
- Break point of view inside a scene
- Use em dashes (ZERO TOLERANCE; use commas, colons, semicolons, or parentheses)
- Resolve the keeper's grief too neatly
- Open on weather alone

## Content Guidelines

### Themes to Emphasize
1. **Solitude versus loneliness**: show the difference through small choices
2. **Duty as a form of love**: render it in tasks, not speeches

### Topics to Handle Carefully
- **Grief**: present, never wallowed in; let it surface in objects and habit

## Character Voice Rules

### Dialogue Guidelines
- **Vocabulary Level**: plain, weathered, economical
- **Speech Patterns**: the keeper speaks rarely and in short lines

### Internal Monologue Style
- **Thought Presentation**: plain, woven into narration
- **Self-Awareness Level**: medium; he notices more than he admits

## Technical Preferences

### Chapter Structure
- **Average Length**: 400 to 700 words for this demo
- **Scene Breaks**: a centered divider line
- **Cliffhangers**: rarely; prefer a quiet turn

### Formatting
- **Dialogue**: US conventions
- **Thoughts**: plain, no italics
- **Punctuation**: NO EM DASHES. Use commas, colons, semicolons, or parentheses.

## Quality Standards

### Minimum Requirements
- Point of view stays consistent within each scene
- The setting is grounded in the first paragraph
- The keeper's grief stays implied, not announced
