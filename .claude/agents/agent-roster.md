# Book Writing Agent Roster

## Complete Agent List (16 Specialists)

### Story Structure Agents
1. **story_architect** - Plot structure, story beats, scene flow and pacing
2. **world_builder** - Setting development and world consistency
3. **character_developer** - Character depth, psychology, and consistency tracking
4. **thematic_guide** - Theme development and symbolic meaning

### Quality Control Agents
5. **continuity_checker** - Master consistency checker for all story elements
6. **timeline_keeper** - Chronological accuracy and logic
7. **style_editor** - Voice consistency and prose quality
8. **grammar_clarity** - Grammar correctness and clarity
9. **dialogue_coach** - Voice distinction and natural speech

### Content Review Agents
10. **sensitivity_reviewer** - Cultural authenticity and representation

### Market & Publishing Agents
11. **market_analyst** - Comprehensive market research, positioning, and audience analysis
12. **publisher_desk** - Publishing submission strategy
13. **query_coach** - Query letters and pitch materials
14. **marketing_strategist** - Marketing campaigns and hooks
15. **critic_sim** - Literary criticism and review prediction
16. **adaptation_scout** - Film/TV adaptation potential

## Agent Capabilities

### Comprehensive Coverage
- **character_developer** handles all character matters including tracking and consistency
- **continuity_checker** validates story compendium and checks all consistency domains
- **story_architect** manages both macro structure and micro scene flow
- **market_analyst** provides complete market research, positioning, and audience analysis

## Collaboration Patterns

### Core Story Team
**Sequential Flow:**
story_architect → character_developer → world_builder → thematic_guide

### Quality Team
**Master Check:**
continuity_checker (coordinates all) → specialized checks as needed

### Market Team
**Integrated Analysis:**
market_analyst (research) → marketing_strategist (execution) → query_coach (materials)
