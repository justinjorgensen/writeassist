# WriteAssist Workshop Questionnaire
*Structured interview guide for story development sessions*

---

## FACILITATOR INSTRUCTIONS
- Record entire session for transcript (expect 90-120 minutes)
- Ask each question clearly
- Probe for specifics when answers are vague
- Note any contradictions for later resolution
- Mark timestamp when switching sections
- Don't skip sections - each feeds the AI configuration

---

## 1. PREMISE & THEMES (15 minutes)

### Core Questions:
**Q1.1:** "In one or two sentences, what is your story about?"
*Listen for: protagonist, problem, stakes*

**Q1.2:** "What genre best describes this story?"
*Probe: Primary genre? Any genre blending?*

**Q1.3:** "What are the 2-3 main themes you want to explore?"
*Listen for: abstract concepts like identity, power, love, justice*

**Q1.4:** "What question does your story ask the reader?"
*Example: "Can someone change their nature?" or "What is the cost of revenge?"*

**Q1.5:** "What's the unique hook that sets your story apart?"
*Probe: What makes this different from others in the genre?*

---

## 2. CHARACTERS (25 minutes)

### Protagonist:
**Q2.1:** "Who is your main character? Name, age, occupation?"

**Q2.2:** "What does your protagonist desperately WANT?"
*External goal - the thing they're pursuing*

**Q2.3:** "What WOUND from their past drives them?"
*The emotional damage that motivates them*

**Q2.4:** "What is their greatest FEAR?"

**Q2.5:** "How will they CHANGE from beginning to end?"
*Their arc - who they are vs who they become*

### Antagonist:
**Q2.6:** "Who or what opposes your protagonist?"

**Q2.7:** "What does the antagonist WANT?"
*Should conflict with protagonist's want*

**Q2.8:** "Why are they justified in their own mind?"
*Their perspective - avoid pure evil*

### Supporting Cast:
**Q2.9:** "Name 3-5 supporting characters and their role:"
*Example: Mentor, ally, love interest, rival*

**Q2.10:** "What does each supporting character want?"

### Character Relationships:
**Q2.11:** "Map the KEY RELATIONSHIPS between characters:"
*Who are allies? Rivals? Love interests? Mentors?*

**Q2.12:** "What relationship will CHANGE the most?"
*Which dynamic shifts from beginning to end?*

**Q2.13:** "What relationship drives the CONFLICT?"
*Which connection creates the most tension?*

---

## 3. SETTING & WORLD RULES (20 minutes)

### Setting:
**Q3.1:** "When and where does your story take place?"
*Specific year/era and location*

**Q3.2:** "What are the 3 most important locations?"
*Where key scenes will happen*

**Q3.3:** "What is the social/political structure?"
*Who has power? What are the conflicts?*

### World Rules:
**Q3.4:** "What are the laws of nature in your world?"
*What's possible? Magic? Technology? Physics?*

**Q3.5:** "What are the LIMITS of power in your world?"
*What CAN'T happen? What's impossible?*

**Q3.6:** "What technology or magic exists? What doesn't?"

**Q3.7:** "What would break your world's believability?"
*The things that must never happen*

---

## 4. PLOT FRAMEWORK (20 minutes)

### Key Plot Points:
**Q4.1:** "What is the INCITING INCIDENT that starts everything?"
*The event that disrupts the status quo*

**Q4.2:** "What is the POINT OF NO RETURN?"
*When the protagonist fully commits*

**Q4.3:** "What happens at the MIDPOINT?"
*The twist/revelation that changes everything*

**Q4.4:** "What is the DARKEST MOMENT?"
*When all seems lost*

**Q4.5:** "Describe the CLIMAX scene:"
*The final confrontation/decision*

**Q4.6:** "What is the ENDING?"
*How is the world/character different?*

**Q4.7:** "What is the READER'S EMOTIONAL JOURNEY?"
*How should they feel at each stage?*

**Q4.8:** "What do you want the reader to FEEL in the final scene?"
*The specific emotion when they close the book*

**Q4.9:** "What QUESTION should linger after the ending?"
*What should they still be thinking about?*

---

## 5. STYLE & BOUNDARIES (15 minutes)

### Technical Choices:
**Q5.1:** "What POINT OF VIEW will you use?"
*First person? Third limited? Multiple POV?*

**Q5.2:** "What TENSE?"
*Past? Present?*

**Q5.3:** "Describe the TONE in 3 words:"
*Example: dark, witty, hopeful*

**Q5.4:** "What is the intended READING LEVEL?"
*YA? Adult? Literary? Commercial?*

### Boundaries & Restrictions:
**Q5.5:** "What will you NEVER do in this story?"
*Example: No head-hopping, no deus ex machina, no sexual violence*

**Q5.6:** "What content restrictions do you have?"
*Violence level? Romance heat? Language?*

**Q5.7:** "What narrative techniques will you AVOID?"
*Dream sequences? Flashbacks? Prophecies?*

**Q5.8:** "What clichés of your genre will you NOT use?"

### Chapter & Scene Requirements:
**Q5.9:** "What RULES must EVERY chapter follow?"
*POV consistency? Minimum conflict? Word count range?*

**Q5.10:** "What must you CHECK before approving any scene?"
*Pacing? Character voice? Plot advancement?*

**Q5.11:** "What would make you REJECT a drafted chapter?"
*What violations would require rewriting?*

---

## 6. CONTINUITY & NAMING (10 minutes)

### Systems:
**Q6.1:** "How does TIME work in your world?"
*Calendar? Seasons? Day/night cycle?*

**Q6.2:** "What NAMING CONVENTIONS will you follow?"
*Character names? Place names? Cultural patterns?*

**Q6.3:** "How do you measure DISTANCE and TRAVEL?"
*Travel times? Geography scale?*

**Q6.4:** "What CURRENCY or ECONOMY exists?"

**Q6.5:** "What LANGUAGES are spoken?"

---

## 7. GLOSSARY & CANON (10 minutes)

### Unique Elements:
**Q7.1:** "List 5-10 unique TERMS in your world:"
*Made-up words, special concepts, titles*

**Q7.2:** "What SYMBOLS or IMAGERY will recur?"
*Example: Ravens, mirrors, specific colors*

**Q7.3:** "What EXISTING CANON must be maintained?"
*If part of a series or shared world*

**Q7.4:** "What are your story's EASTER EGGS?"
*Hidden references or foreshadowing*

---

## 8. OPEN QUESTIONS (5 minutes)

### To Be Determined:
**Q8.1:** "What aspects are you still UNSURE about?"

**Q8.2:** "What would you like to DISCOVER while writing?"

**Q8.3:** "What might CHANGE as you develop the story?"

**Q8.4:** "What questions do you want READERS to debate?"

---

## 9. METADATA & GOALS (15 minutes)

### Project Specifications:
**Q9.1:** "What is your target WORD COUNT?"

**Q9.2:** "How many CHAPTERS do you envision?"

**Q9.3:** "What is your DEADLINE or timeline?"

**Q9.4:** "Is this a STANDALONE or part of a SERIES?"

**Q9.5:** "Who is your TARGET AUDIENCE?"

### Publishing & Market Intent:
**Q9.6:** "What are your PUBLISHING GOALS?"
*Traditional? Self-publish? Hybrid? Series potential?*

**Q9.7:** "What's your MARKET POSITIONING?"
*Comparable titles? "It's X meets Y"?*

**Q9.8:** "What makes this MARKETABLE?"
*Hook? Trend? Unique angle?*

### Inspiration & Voice:
**Q9.9:** "What books/films INSPIRE this story's tone?"
*List 3-5 works that capture the feeling you want*

**Q9.10:** "Which AUTHORS influence your style?"
*Whose voice do you admire and why?*

**Q9.11:** "What's your UNIQUE VOICE?"
*What makes your writing distinctly yours?*

## 10. QUALITY GATES & ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA (10 minutes)

### WRP Acceptance Rules:
**Q10.1:** "What must EVERY WRP include?"
*Specific elements required in each chapter plan*

**Q10.2:** "What would FAIL a WRP review?"
*Missing elements? Continuity breaks? Pacing issues?*

**Q10.3:** "What chapter patterns do you want to MAINTAIN?"
*Structure? Cliffhangers? Scene count?*

### Success Metrics:
**Q10.4:** "How will you know a chapter is READY?"
*Word count? Conflict resolution? Character movement?*

**Q10.5:** "What quality score is your MINIMUM?"
*What quality bar should each chapter clear before it counts as done?*

**Q10.6:** "What would trigger a REWRITE vs a REVISION?"
*When to fix vs start over?*
